Three places a buyer could stop

Can my company use Basic?

The pricing card says “For personal use cases”. The table below it says Commercial, and the Enterprise FAQ says every paid plan has a commercial licence.

Try: “Small projects, commercial use included.”

When is upgrading cheaper?

At yearly prices with overage at $0.0005 a call, I worked out the points: Basic to Analyst at 248,400 calls a month, Analyst to Lite at 1,092,000, Lite to Pro at 2,800,000. I didn't find them in the pricing page text.

Try: show the point beside each plan.

How big are you?

The Enterprise page says 10 billion API calls a month. The job ad says more than 45 billion. The docs home says 220+ networks; pricing says 360+.

Check: these may measure different things. If they don't, a large buyer sees the smaller one.

The fix: route the account before it stops

It projects each account's month, compares overage with the next plan, and picks who should act. If the data can't be trusted, it stops. Choose an account and change the signals.

What this is, and isn't

  • It is plain rules you can read and argue with, running in this page. No AI decides here. AI's part would be reading free-text enquiries into these same fields.
  • It assumes yearly list prices without the current sale, one credit per call and a steady month. The $300 a month cut-off for a seller is my guess.
  • I haven't seen your CRM, usage data or sales process. You may already do this.
